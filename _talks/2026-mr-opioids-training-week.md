  opioids, we used a research-ready peri-operative dataset to measure
  prescribing across more than 100,000 elective surgical cases at UCLH. The talk
  shows how routine data pipelines can support a rapid response to a safety
  alert, and how the findings fed into an electronic health record prompt to
  support safer prescribing.
