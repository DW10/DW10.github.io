summary: >
  Total opioid burden (OME/MME) is a widely used outcome, appearing in over
  4,000 papers in five years, yet the conversion values behind it rest on a thin
  evidence base. Two simulation studies show that bias depends on the mix of
  opioids given and the error in the conversion values used, and an exploratory
  literature search finds that methods are poorly reported and difficult to
  reproduce. Studies should report mean doses for each opioid alongside the
  conversion values used.
