summary: >
  There's a saying in anaesthesia: ask three anaesthetists how to put a patient
  to sleep, and you'll get five different answers. This talk explores when
  variation in practice fits care to the patient, and when it reflects genuine
  uncertainty (equipoise) about which option is best.


  Drawing on the learning health system, it introduces decision architecture
  randomised trials (DART), which use nudges in the electronic health record to
  generate evidence from routine care, and sets out a PhD roadmap: find
  variation with true equipoise, prioritise the questions that matter, and use
  DART to answer them.
